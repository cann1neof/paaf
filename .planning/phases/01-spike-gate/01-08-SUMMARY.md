# 01-08 — Blind native-ear Hebrew verdict

## Self-Check: PASSED

The one deliverable exists and was produced correctly:
`raw/hebrew-verdict.md` holds verbatim per-clip impressions, judgment was blind
(`mapping.json` joined back only after all 46 impressions were typed), Hebrew was judged
deeply and Russian/Spanish spot-checked, and no fallback rung was locked (D-10 lean only).

## What happened

This plan is the irreducibly human core of the phase (D-13). The project owner listened to
all 46 blind-shuffled clips in `corpus/clips_blind/`, typed a free-text impression for each
into `raw/hebrew-judgments.json` (blank template keyed by opaque blind name — no tags
visible), and only afterward ran `shuffle_blind.py join` to attach the real `language_id`
tags, producing `raw/hebrew-verdict.md`. 46/46 clips carry an impression.

Executor role here was bookkeeping only: the verdict is the human's, recorded verbatim.
The synthesis into RESULTS/DECISIONS happened alongside 01-10.

## The verdict

**Hebrew is usable.** Of the 7 Hebrew clips: 2 explicitly good ("goood hebrew!", "very very
good one mix of languages"), 4 unremarkable-acceptable, 1 partial breakdown (c08 — Hebrew and
English intelligible, then an embedded stretch degrades to non-word babble "psh phs"). No
total Hebrew failure. This is materially better than the LOW-confidence "may be embarrassing"
prior CLAUDE.md carried, and it means the founding four-language property holds on this evidence.

**D-20 spot-check surfaced two catastrophic non-Hebrew clips** (recorded now, not buried for
Phase 7): c20 Russian ("not close to anything") and c03 Spanish ("psh psh, not real words" —
the same babble mode as c08). Plus an accent-bleed note on Russian c16 (one accent applied
across both languages). All are inputs to Phase 6/7, not resolved here.

**D-05 binds:** this is synthetic speech; a Chatterbox-vs-Whisper attribution is not possible,
and all Russian was rendered without stress labeling (permanent PyPI 404).
