<objective>
Render the failure-grid corpus through Chatterbox Multilingual V3 into the ~40 clips that SPIKE-01
(code-switching), SPIKE-02 (Hebrew judgment), and SPIKE-04 (Whisper) all consume. Each sentence is
rendered twice — matrix-tag and embedded-tag (D-06) — plus a free `language_id=None` control condition

subprocesses so the Apple-Silicon memory leak cannot corrupt the timing series (Pitfall 5). Then produce
the blind-shuffled copies + mapping (D-14) that the native-ear judgment will consume.

This plan runs only AFTER Gate 0 is green (01-03) — Hebrew must not be synthesized for judgment through a
degraded config.
