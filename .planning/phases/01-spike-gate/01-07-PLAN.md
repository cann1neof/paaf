<objective>
Measure whether Whisper locks onto one language and transliterates or translates the rest, on the SAME
corpus clips (D-04). faster-whisper is the only STT engine exposing the instrument the lock-on question
needs: a `multilingual=True` "detect language on every segment" flag plus `all_language_probs` and a
`detect_language()` call. The result carries a binding caveat (D-05): because Whisper is fed Chatterbox
output, this measures "Whisper on synthetic mixed speech," and Chatterbox failures and Whisper failures
are not separable here — that caveat must travel with the result into DECISIONS.md.

Purpose: answer SPIKE-04's lock-on / transliterate / translate question with the right instrument, and
correct the old "STT is a non-issue" claim before it reaches a README (SC#4).
Output: per-clip transcripts + language posteriors + a raw write-up carrying the D-05 caveat.
</objective>
