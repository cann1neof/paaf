<objective>
Make "independently installable" un-rottable (SC#2, FOUND-02): a separate CI job builds the `paaf-llm` wheel, installs it into a clean venv with no workspace present, and runs its smoke tests — proving `paaf-llm` pulls in neither voice nor audio dependencies. The negative control (assert `paaf_voice` is NOT importable in the clean venv) is what makes the job fail the instant a hidden coupling is introduced (Pitfall 2).

Phase Goal (developer-as-user story): **As a** developer who will `pip install paaf-llm` in Phase 5, **I want** CI to prove the wheel stands alone today, **so that** the standalone-package promise cannot silently rot before the PyPI release.

Purpose: FOUND-02 is the mechanical guarantee behind Phase 5's "a stranger pip installs paaf-llm." A `pip install -e` or PYTHONPATH leak would make it pass falsely; this plan forces the built-wheel path.
Output: A build+clean-venv+smoke script, a `--pyargs`-importable smoke test, and the `wheel-independence` CI job.
</objective>
